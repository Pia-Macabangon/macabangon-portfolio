PROJECT 03 SYSTEM DESIGN • HARDWARE INTEGRATION

Self-Service Printing Kiosk.

A guided, self-contained terminal intended to assist users through document selection, hybrid digital payment, and automated dispatch.

01 • CONCEPT

The Core Idea

A self-service kiosk concept built for campus corridors and student libraries. The kiosk guides customers through USB/cloud document uploads, print configurations, payment processing, and job spooling without human supervision.

02 • SUBSYSTEMS

System Architecture

  • Host Computing: Raspberry Pi 4 running dedicated kiosk environment
  • Touch Interface: Responsive touchscreen layout with clear large targets
  • Payment Subsystem: Hybrid cash validator and QR-based e-wallet reader
  • Mechanical & Power: Isolated power rails with surge and thermal protections
03 • CRAFT

Engineering Focus

Partitioning a complex multi-device system into distinct subsystems: power distribution, controller communications, payment security, and paper queue management. High focus was placed on fail-safe paper jam recovery and reliable kiosk lockdown.

04 • STATUS

Current Milestones

System design blueprints and user flow completed. Enclosure 3D modeling and peripheral driver scripting currently underway in computer engineering laboratory work.
